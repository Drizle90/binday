Recycling Guide

What goes in each bin

General Waste

YES: Food scraps, nappies, broken glass, dirty packaging, polystyrene, soft plastics (in some councils).

NO: Recyclables, garden waste, batteries, electronics, hazardous waste.

Recycling

YES: Clean paper, cardboard, glass bottles, aluminium cans, hard plastics (1, 2 & 5), milk cartons.

NO: Soft plastics, food waste, polystyrene, nappies, plastic bags, broken glass.

Tip: Rinse containers and don't bag your recycling.

Garden / Organics

YES: Lawn clippings, leaves, small branches, weeds, prunings.

FOGO councils also: Food scraps, fruit/veg peels, coffee grounds, eggshells.

NO: Plastic bags, soil, rocks, treated timber, large branches.

💡 When in doubt, leave it out — putting wrong items in recycling can contaminate the whole truckload.
