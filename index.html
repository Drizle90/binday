Honest answers

Is it really free?

Yes - completely free, with no ads, no signup, and no premium tier. Built as a personal project to fix a real annoyance.

Does it pull real council data?

No - Australia doesn't have a unified council bin API. You enter your collection day once and the app calculates the rest using standard fortnightly rotation. This is accurate for the vast majority of households.

Will it send me reminders?

Not directly yet - true scheduled push notifications need a server. The best workaround is the "Add to your calendar" button, which exports a year of bin nights as an .ics file with built-in reminders. Your phone's calendar will then nag you the night before.

Does it work on iPhone & Android?

Yes - works in any modern browser. You can also "Add to Home Screen" so it behaves like a native app.

What about my data?

Everything stays on your device in browser storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clear your browser data and it's gone.
